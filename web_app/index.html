Heavy models

Most systems need dedicated GPUs and cloud streaming, which adds delay and recurring cost.

Flickering output

The in-between motion from one sign to the next causes erratic, wrong predictions (movement epenthesis).

straighten

Distance sensitivity

Prediction results drift and degrade when you move closer to or further from the camera feed.
